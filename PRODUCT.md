# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Fluxora is for serious Skyrim modders maintaining large local builds, Nexus Mods workflows, Mod Organizer 2 migrations, VFS launch setups, plugin order, archive installs, FluxPack packages, and build-aware troubleshooting.

## Product Purpose

Fluxora is a fast, native-first desktop mod manager. The product succeeds when users can inspect, maintain, launch, package, transfer, and diagnose large builds without losing trust in what the app is doing to their files or profiles.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exacting, capable. Fluxora should feel like an operational tool for careful power users: dense enough for real work, restrained enough to stay readable, and polished enough that users trust risky mod-management flows.

## Anti-references

Do not make Fluxora feel like a marketing dashboard, decorative launcher, game-themed skin, glassy concept UI, or generic AI-generated control panel. Avoid ornamental cards, vague feature copy, needless gradients, mismatched icon families, and UI that exposes filesystem or bridge complexity through unclear controls.

## Design Principles

- Keep the interface task-first: visible controls should map to actions users can take now.
- Preserve the Tauri app shell and shared primitives before inventing new component shapes.
- Make state and capability limits explicit without crowding the workspace.
- Use restrained color: gold is for primary actions, selection, and meaningful state.
- Prefer consistency over novelty so repeated mod-management workflows become muscle memory.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Preserve keyboard focus, labels, reduced-motion behavior, stable hit targets, and text that fits at supported desktop window sizes. Keep icon-only controls labeled with accessible names or tooltips.
